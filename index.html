Ginkgo Biloba
Cerebral Circulation

Ginkgo biloba has decades of research behind its ability to improve blood flow to the brain. Studies show it inhibits platelet aggregation and supports vasodilation, delivering more oxygen and nutrients to neurons. Many users of this better brain memory formula report noticeable improvements in mental sharpness within weeks of starting Ginkgo-containing supplements.

Acetyl-L-Carnitine
Mitochondrial Energy

Neurons are among the most energy-demanding cells in the body. Acetyl-L-Carnitine helps shuttle fatty acids into mitochondria for energy conversion, directly supporting the fuel supply to brain cells. Clinical evidence indicates it may reduce mental fatigue and support recall speed, particularly in adults over 50.

Rhodiola Rosea
Stress Adaptogen

Chronic stress is one of the leading drivers of memory loss, largely through the damaging effects of excess cortisol on the hippocampus. Rhodiola Rosea is a well-documented adaptogen that reduces stress hormone activity and supports mental endurance. Research suggests it improves cognitive performance under fatigue-inducing conditions.

Vitamin B Complex (B6, B9, B12)
Homocysteine Control

Elevated homocysteine levels are strongly associated with accelerated brain aging and memory decline. B vitamins are the primary dietary factors that regulate homocysteine metabolism. Studies show that supplementing B6, folate, and B12 in combination significantly reduces homocysteine and supports long-term cognitive health in older adults.

Alpha GPC
Acetylcholine Precursor

Alpha GPC is one of the most bioavailable choline sources available in supplement form. The brain uses choline to synthesize acetylcholine, the neurotransmitter directly responsible for attention, learning, and memory formation. Research consistently ranks Alpha GPC among the most effective nootropic ingredients for short-term memory improvement.
